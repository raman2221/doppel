What it handles

Everything you'd rather not do.

Inbox, in your voice.

How it works

Ten minutes to set up.
Then just say what you need.

  1. 1

    Connect once

    Add a Claude API key, connect your email with an app password, and let Doppel read your sent mail to learn your style.

  2. 2

    Ask in one line

    “Clear my inbox.” “Fill out this lease.” “Find a table for two on Friday.” No prompt writing. It plans the steps itself.

  3. 3

    Stay in the loop

    Watch every step as it happens. Approve what matters, stop it any time, and resume later without anything being sent twice.

Safety

Trust is the feature.

Never types your secrets

Social Security numbers, passwords, and card or bank numbers are always left for you, in forms, on websites, and in memory.

Stops at checkout

It finds the item and fills in the details, then tells you what's in the cart and the total. You pay.

30-minute undo

Archived mail, saved drafts, filled forms, reminders, and memory changes can all be reversed.

A log that can't be quietly edited

Every action is chained to the one before it with SHA-256. One click checks that nothing was changed.

Keys stay in your keychain

Your API key and email password are encrypted by your operating system and kept out of every prompt.

Your data stays home

Memory, tasks, and history live on your computer. Only the task you're working on is sent to Claude.

Opening it for the first time

Mac: Doppel isn't notarized by Apple yet, so macOS asks before opening it. Drag Doppel to Applications and open it. If macOS blocks it, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.

Windows: If SmartScreen appears, click More info, then Run anyway.

Then open Settings in Doppel and paste a Claude API key from console.anthropic.com.

Questions.

Is Doppel free?

Yes. The app is free and open source. Doppel thinks with Claude, so you add your own API key and pay Anthropic directly for what you use, usually a few cents per task.

What leaves my computer?

Only what the current task needs is sent to Claude, like the emails you asked it to sort. Your memory, history, and activity log stay on your computer. Your keys and passwords are never included in what's sent.

Will it send emails without asking?

Only if you choose “Don't ask”, and even then each email shows for a few seconds so you can cancel. The default mode waits for your approval before anything leaves your computer.

Which email providers work?

Gmail, Outlook, iCloud, and most others that support IMAP. You connect with an app password rather than your regular password. Some school and work accounts turn app passwords off.

Does it run on Intel Macs?

The download is built for Apple Silicon. Intel Macs can run it from source with npm install and npm start.

Why does my computer warn me when I open it?

Signing an app with Apple and Microsoft costs money, and Doppel's builds aren't signed yet. The code is public on GitHub, so you can read exactly what you're running.
