Questions

Is Doppel free?

Yes, and it's open source. Doppel thinks with Claude, so you add your own API key and pay Anthropic directly for what you use, usually a few cents per task.

What leaves my computer?

Only what the current task needs is sent to Claude, like the emails you asked it to sort. Your memory, history, and activity log stay on your computer, and your keys are never sent.

Will it send emails without asking?

Only in “Don't ask” mode, and even then each email shows for a few seconds so you can cancel. By default, anything leaving your computer waits for you.

Which email providers work?

Gmail, Outlook, iCloud, and most providers with IMAP. You connect with an app password, not your regular one. Some school and work accounts turn app passwords off.

Will it run on my older or smaller Mac?

The download is built for Apple Silicon (M1 or later) on macOS 12 or newer, and it's tuned for 8 GB of memory. Intel Macs can run it from source.

Why does my computer warn me the first time?

The builds aren't signed with Apple or Microsoft yet. On a Mac, open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. On Windows, click More info, then Run anyway. The code is public, so you can see exactly what you're running.
